## Leakage and repetition

- All 800 public task texts were unique after the repository's normalization, with zero exact matches against the registered historical set. A rendered-prompt scan found zero literal private terminal `value`, `limit`, `draft_id` or `audit_ref` strings. Public escalation reason codes and L0 reference or clarification fields are part of the task contract. The exact-match and substring checks leave paraphrase similarity unadjudicated. [Historical filter](../benchmarks/slm10/planning_roster.py)
- L1 through L7 reuse 100 distinct references across 700 cases. An exact lookup table learned from the two L1 worlds alone recovered the terminal value in 800 of 800 checked L2–L5 worlds. The test matched the ordinary and alternate conditions and excluded revision and denial worlds. It measures predictability under hypothetical exposure to another level's controls. [Public reference generation](../benchmarks/slm10/planning_public_authoring.py) · [Private fixture values](../benchmarks/slm10/planning_source_controls.py)
- Each level uses one exact public tool-contract vocabulary and schema across its 100 cases. Ordered tool/dependency shapes reduce to one per level except L6, which has ten shapes from the 11–20-call range. Business labels and three language strata provide variation around those shared structures. Case counts support coverage estimates within these authored strata. [Public authoring](../benchmarks/slm10/planning_public_authoring.py) · [World and witness authoring](../benchmarks/slm10/planning_source_controls.py)
- The 100 synthetic L7 approval token strings follow a fixed derivation from the public subject. The wrong-draft mutations confirm that knowing a string still cannot replace the trusted host event in source validation. The derivation makes these fixtures unsuitable for measuring unpredictable credential handling. [Approval fixture](../benchmarks/slm10/planning_source_controls.py) · [External event authority](../benchmarks/agentic_planning_v2/events.py)

Before release review, resolve the L5 escalation contract and require the L6 source validator to bind both staged fields to their authoritative inputs. Use disjoint reference namespaces and fixture value assignments across levels or account for their dependence in the study design. Have an independent reviewer assess semantic overlap and provenance, and keep the 300 repaired worlds as regression controls. [Roster release gates](../docs/SLM10_PLANNING_ROSTER.md)
